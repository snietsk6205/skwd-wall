In short:
- Screen: the fastest frames came 35.1 ms apart (about 28 Hz); 94 % of the frames while moving were ready for the next refresh. Each frame (CPU + GPU) must be ready within one refresh, or it waits for the one after (that halves the frame rate).
- Moving with the arrow keys: 26 fps (half the frames 39.1 ms apart or less).
- The same without the FPS cap (general.maxFps limit): 27 fps.
- Settings click, new way (saved in the background): shows on screen after 1.4 ms (slowest 1.4 ms).
- Settings click, old way (saved on the UI thread, as before M1): 3.1 ms (slowest 55.3 ms).
- Layout switch: the slowest first frame was 9.2 ms (coverflow; renderer 7.5 ms of it); the slowest frame after a switch 285.5 ms (hand).
Goals: idle 0 redraws; smooth >= 95 %; a settings click on screen within 2 refreshes; a layout's first frame under 50 ms.
